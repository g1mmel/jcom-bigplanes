# claude-design — Gunship visual reference review

Reviewed 29 October 2026. Scope: visible surfaces and game animation only. These photographs were downloaded from their original public DVIDS preview URLs and actually inspected; they are not generated imagery. The full file hashes and claim-level limits are in [reference-ledger.json](reference-ledger.json).

## Required corrections

- Treat the forward system as a single-barrel GAU-23/A-style installation: dark barrel, enlarged vented muzzle sleeve, circular flexible pressure boot and fuselage surround. The receiver, feed housing and catch bags belong inside.
- Keep 30-mm spent cases and links inside collection bags. Model cases as hollow brass objects separate from outgoing projectile effects.
- The earlier 105-mm installation has visible recoil and a substantial internal cradle with yellow rear rails. Its empty cases remain inside the aircraft.
- Use discrete muzzle flashes and dissipating smoke as authored visual effects. Persistent rocket plumes and Gatling barrel rotation are inappropriate for these selected gunship systems.
- Preserve the circa-2021 candidate choice. NAVSEA announced a replacement 105-mm Gun Aircraft Unit in January 2022; later geometry must be a separate configuration.

## Evidence limits

The images establish visible shape features, where components live, and qualitative motion. They do not establish engineering dimensions, real weapon operating limits, tactical behavior or certified firing cadence. The 2019 aft view confirms the earlier J 105-mm exterior projection, but the gun occupies too few pixels to accept every muzzle detail. The game revision must retain that limitation.

The 2016 image DVIDS-2850910 has inconsistent date text: page metadata says September 6; caption says September 8. No precision claim depends on the two-day difference.

### Sensor configuration

The [USAF J fact sheet](https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/467756/ac-130j-ghostrider/) confirms two EO/IR sensors. A [USAF account of the W predecessor](https://www.robins.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/377994/mc-130w-dragon-spear-visits-robins/) identifies two MX-20 units. DOT&E's [FY2020 report](https://www.dote.osd.mil/Portals/97/pub/reports/FY2020/other/2020DOTEAnnualReport.pdf?ver=rvLsaCQ_njLmPDrNIFJBWQ%3D%3D) mentions MX-20 and MX-25 on the J. Exact sensor designation and lens assignment are therefore configuration-dependent and remain open for this candidate.

### 105-mm generation

[NAVSEA, January 11, 2022](https://www.navsea.navy.mil/Media/News/Article/2895381/nswc-dahlgren-engineers-deliver-upgraded-gun-aircraft-unit-for-the-warfighters/) describes a replacement gun. The 2017 and 2021 interior references here are deliberately earlier, and show that the yellow rails are valid for that earlier visual configuration.
