# Reference and acquisition notes

Author: claude-design / cg46. The image ledger contains twelve inspected public
DVIDS photographs. They support visible system form, port-side placement,
internal case handling and earlier gun installation details. They are not
manufacturing drawings or proof of our authored motion timings.

The 30 mm exterior reference has one barrel, a vented end sleeve and a broad
flexible circular seal. Internal images show the receiver and feed equipment
inside the cabin, with empty cases falling into collection bags. Earlier
105 mm photographs show the dark gun/cradle and yellow rear rails; separate
images show recoil and spent cases retained inside the aircraft.

The 105 mm muzzle contour remains approximate because the selected dated
external view is not a close-up. Exact J EO/IR type and station remain open.
The independent sensor is an MX-20-family representative visual model guided
by manufacturer imagery and the dated aircraft chin photograph.
