## Unified outputs

The \`family/r0001/\` directory is generated by \`tools/build_c130_family.py\` and checked by \`tools/validate_c130_family.py\`.

- \`c130-hercules-unified-r0001.glb\`: the common legacy airframe and four-blade propeller nodes, with no mission kit.
- \`ac130-hercules-unified-r0001.glb\`: **the exact same source airframe**, plus two separate port-side gun **proxies**. These are not certified AC-130H/U station geometry.
- \`ac130j-ghostrider-unified-r0001.glb\`: **the same source airframe**, six-blade game-approximation propellers and independent 30 mm/105 mm gun and EO/IR turret proxies. These are **not** photo-matched AC-130J equipment, and J-specific engines/avionics/silhouette remain open.

Every file records \`C130_COMMON_r0001\` and the same original source SHA-256. The common source's 24 original mesh definitions remain unchanged; only variant visibility, coordinate normalization, and additional mission-kit meshes/nodes differ.

### Part and animation structure

\`C130_FAMILY_ROOT\` owns \`AIRFRAME_COMMON_SOURCE\`, optional \`PROP_LEGACY_4BLADE\`, \`MISSION_KIT_*\`, and \`SOCKETS_GAME_ONLY\`. The J candidate has four named \`PROP_N_SPIN_PIVOT\` nodes. These are attachment/control metadata only: no native animated clips or clearance sweep have been tested.

All socket positions are **game alignment placeholders**. In particular \`SOCKET_RECEIVER_UARRSI\` does not establish a real receptacle's placement, and the tanker delivery sockets do not establish tanker hardware. **No universal receiver probe, retractable probe, cover cavity or tanker boom was invented.** AC-130J receiver geometry requires exact-variant photographs before authoring.

### Coordinate convention

Metres, X = lateral, Y = longitudinal (nose toward negative Y), Z = up. The original source is preserved in its original coordinate frame under one common parent transform.

### Unbuilt follow-on configurations

MC-130, HC-130, KC-130, EC/E-130, LC-130, WC-130 and fictional XC-130J **are not produced in r0001**. Their mission kits, era-specific airframe differences, refueling receivers versus tanker delivery systems, ski equipment, and fictional Fulton/Skyhook/JATO require independent sourced modules and review. The fictional XC-130J is AC-130J + Fulton-derived Skyhook + JATO, not a real certified configuration.

### Outstanding gates

- Photo-calibrated front/side/top and quarter-view geometry comparisons, especially cockpit/nose, nacelles, wing, tail, sponsons and doors.
- Replace proxy mission equipment with verified shape and correct variant placement.
- Confirm original model licensing: \`sketchfab-downloader\` generator does **not** prove redistribution rights.
- Rig pivots, control curves, deployed/stowed states, interior access, damage levels, LODs, collision, materials, texture appearance, shadows.
- Native Blender edit/reopen and Unreal import/cook/runtime: **NOT RUN**.
- The C-130/XC-130 lane remains excluded from **Crowd asset migration** unless the owner explicitly changes that restriction. These files are review candidates on a separate \`jcom-bigplanes\` GitHub branch, not Crowd asset ingestion.
